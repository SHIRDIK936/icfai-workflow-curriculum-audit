# Task 1 — Time Savings and ROI Analysis

## 1. Evidence Status

All figures in this analysis are hypothetical assumptions.

No real ICFAI laboratory booking timings, request volumes
or operational costs were measured.

## 2. Assumed Manual Processing Time

| Activity | Minutes |
|---|---:|
| Student prepares request | 10 |
| Faculty checks request | 5 |
| Laboratory availability check | 5 |
| Approval communication | 5 |
| Total | 25 |

Total manual effort = 25 minutes per request.

## 3. Assumed Digital Processing Time

| Activity | Minutes |
|---|---:|
| Student completes online form | 3 |
| Administrative review and approval | 5 |
| Total | 8 |

Total digital effort = 8 minutes per request.

## 4. Gross Time Savings

Manual effort = 25 minutes

Digital effort = 8 minutes

Gross savings per request:

25 - 8 = 17 minutes

Assumed monthly request volume = 100

Monthly gross savings:

100 × 17 = 1,700 minutes

1,700 / 60 = 28.33 hours

## 5. Rework Assumptions

Assumed manual correction rate = 20%

Additional effort per correction = 10 minutes

Manual rework:

100 × 0.20 × 10 = 200 minutes/month

Assumed digital correction rate = 5%

Additional effort per correction = 5 minutes

Digital rework:

100 × 0.05 × 5 = 25 minutes/month

Additional savings from reduced rework:

200 - 25 = 175 minutes/month

## 6. Monthly Support and Review

Assumed additional digital support = 120 minutes/month

Assumed additional administrative review = 60 minutes/month

Total additional effort:

120 + 60 = 180 minutes/month

## 7. Net Monthly Savings

Gross savings = 1,700 minutes

Rework savings = 175 minutes

Additional support and review = 180 minutes

Net savings:

1,700 + 175 - 180 = 1,695 minutes/month

1,695 / 60 = 28.25 hours/month

## 8. Implementation Effort

Assumed one-time implementation effort = 60 hours

Monthly net savings = 28.25 hours

Estimated effort payback:

60 / 28.25 = 2.12 months

This is an effort-based estimate, not a financial ROI.

## 9. Volume Sensitivity

Assuming the same correction rates and fixed
monthly support/review effort:

Net savings = 18.75 × monthly requests - 180 minutes

| Requests/Month | Net Minutes | Net Hours |
|---|---:|---:|
| 50 | 757.5 | 12.63 |
| 100 | 1695 | 28.25 |
| 150 | 2632.5 | 43.88 |

## 10. Limitations

- All processing times are assumed.
- Monthly request volume is unverified.
- Correction rates are hypothetical.
- Implementation effort is an estimate.
- No actual staff time savings have been measured.
- The estimate assumes successful adoption.
- Maintenance and infrastructure costs are not monetized.
- Real operational results may differ substantially.

## 11. Conclusion

Under the stated assumptions, the proposed workflow
could save approximately 28.25 hours of combined
student and administrative effort per month at
100 requests.

This is a hypothetical estimate requiring validation
before any implementation decision.
