# Project Limitations

**Review date:** 24 October 2026

## Task 1

1. The laboratory booking workflow is hypothetical.
2. No authorized field observation or staff interview was conducted.
3. Request volume and processing times are estimates.
4. Five synthetic tests do not establish production reliability.
5. Security, concurrency, database recovery, and real approval procedures require further testing.
6. The proposed workflow has not received institutional approval.

## Task 2

1. The curriculum transcription is based on the supplied handbook page.
2. Course-credit sums and printed totals contain unresolved discrepancies.
3. Semesters VII and VIII contain grouped activities with unallocated credits in the CSV.
4. The redesign is provisional and is not certified as a complete credit-balanced programme.
5. The three job descriptions are from one employer and do not represent the entire job market.
6. No faculty or graduate validation has been recorded.
7. The five Python tests verify reference solutions, not actual AI-generated responses.
8. The proposed database laboratory has not been delivered to students.
9. The Streamlit dashboard is an academic prototype, not an institutional information system.

## Required Future Validation

- Confirm the official regulations and complete semester credit structure.
- Obtain permission before studying a real college workflow.
- Collect dated observations and authorized stakeholder feedback.
- Capture and independently assess actual AI-generated exercise responses.
- Validate curriculum changes with relevant faculty and graduates.
- Review privacy, security, accessibility, and deployment requirements before any real implementation.

## Conclusion

The repository demonstrates an academic investigation, provisional redesign, and synthetic testing. It does not claim institutional approval, real-world deployment, or independently measured operational benefits.
